About Us

Traditional bakery values with premium quality standards

Imperial Crown is a premium bakery brand proudly bringing the authentic taste of Armenian lavash tradition to the UAE. Our production is based in Sharjah, with delivery available across all Emirates.

We combine traditional Armenian baking heritage with modern production standards to create high-quality products for both retail and wholesale customers. Our lavash is made with premium flour, pure Armenian water, and a yeast-free recipe that reflects authenticity, care, and exceptional quality.

Our Philosophy

We believe bakery products should represent more than daily food. They should reflect culture, craftsmanship, quality, and trust. Imperial Crown was created with the vision of offering bakery products that meet the highest standards while preserving authentic Armenian taste and tradition.

Our Quality

Every detail matters to us — from ingredient selection to production quality, presentation, and delivery. Our goal is to build a bakery brand known for premium quality, consistency, and long-term partnerships across the UAE.
