About Imperial Crown

Premium Armenian Lavash Brand

Our Story

Imperial Crown is a premium bakery brand created to represent authentic Armenian lavash with modern production standards and elegant presentation in the UAE.

Authentic Armenian Tradition

Our bakery is inspired by centuries of Armenian baking tradition. Lavash is one of the oldest breads in the world and an important part of Armenian culture.

Imperial Crown brings this tradition to the UAE with premium ingredients, careful production and reliable supply.

Premium Quality Standards

We use carefully selected flour and pure Armenian water to produce yeast-free lavash with authentic taste and texture.

Our goal is to build a bakery brand that represents quality, clean production and long-term trust.
