About Imperial Crown

Premium Armenian Bakery in the UAE

Our Story

Imperial Crown was created to bring authentic Armenian lavash to the United Arab Emirates. Our goal is to combine traditional Armenian bakery recipes with modern production standards and premium quality.

Our Production

Our production is located in Sharjah. We supply fresh Armenian lavash to restaurants, supermarkets, hotels and distributors across the UAE.

Our Philosophy

Imperial Crown is not just a bakery. It is a premium bakery brand where quality, tradition and authenticity come first.
